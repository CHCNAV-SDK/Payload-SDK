# Development and Testing
When developing applications with PSDK, observe the following to protect developers from accidents:
- When developing or testing applications based on PSDK, remove the propellers from the aircraft
- Do not approach the aircraft while the motors are spinning
- Do not input high-power current into the aircraft power output interface
# Pre-Flight Checklist
To help prevent aircraft damage or safety incidents caused by application failures, unexpected events, or improper operations, check the following before running the application:
- Correctly set the baud rate and port name for the program on the onboard computer or third-party development platform
- Ensure that the aircraft and remote controller battery levels are above 50%
- Confirm that the aircraft status is normal as required by the user manual
# Flight Environment
- Do not use CHCNAV aircraft or applications developed with PSDK in extreme weather such as strong wind, rain, or snow
- Do not operate the aircraft in densely built-up areas or crowded environments
- Do not fly near high-voltage lines, communication base stations, or transmission towers, to avoid wireless interference with the remote controller
- When flying at high altitude, aircraft performance may be affected by environmental factors; fly with caution
